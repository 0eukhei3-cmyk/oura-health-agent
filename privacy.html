Privacy Policy

This is a personal application intended solely for use by its owner.

The application accesses Oura health data only with the account owner's authorisation, for personal health tracking and analysis.

The application does not sell health data or provide it to advertisers.

Data may be processed by third-party AI services if the owner enables AI analysis.

Data access can be revoked through the connected Oura account. Stored copies can be deleted by the application owner.
